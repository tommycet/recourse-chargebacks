⚠ The Problem

x402 Has No Chargebacks

The x402 protocol processes AI agent micropayments before delivery is verified. Once USDC leaves the buyer's wallet, there's no on-chain mechanism to dispute or reverse the transaction.

The x402 specification defines a 4-step payment flow: request, payment required, authorization, settlement. No dispute mechanism. No delivery verification. No chargeback. Once settled, the payment is final. — x402 Protocol Specification v2, §2 Core Payment Flow
