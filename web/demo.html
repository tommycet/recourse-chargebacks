⚠ The Problem
The x402 specification defines a 4-step payment flow: request → payment required → authorization → settlement. No dispute mechanism. No delivery verification. No chargeback. Once settled, the payment is final — even if the seller delivered nothing.
— x402 Protocol Specification v2, §2 Core Payment Flow
Why Recourse?

x402 has no chargebacks. The protocol processes payments before delivery is verified — once funds leave the buyer's wallet, there's no on-chain mechanism to dispute or reverse the transaction.

Recourse adds escrow-backed safety. Funds are held in a smart contract until the buyer confirms delivery or raises a dispute. Cryptographic evidence bundles capture the full request/response payload as keccak256 hashes committed on-chain.

AI arbiter resolves disputes at machine speed. When a dispute is raised, an on-chain arbiter reviews the evidence and issues a binding refund or payout — no lawyers, no chargebacks, no waiting.
